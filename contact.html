Frequently Asked Questions

When does Million Tribe launch?
Million Tribe officially launches on January 1, 2027. Join the waitlist now to secure your spot and receive 225 TRIBE Tokens on launch day.
How much does membership cost?
$1 per month. This gives you access to the monthly Wealth Builder e-magazine, TRIBE token rewards, community access, and a complimentary $1M sweepstakes draw entry.
Do I need to purchase to win?
No. No purchase is necessary to enter or win. A free alternative mail-in method of entry is available. See our No Purchase Necessary page for details.
When does the TRIBE Token launch?
TRIBE Token launches no earlier than 6 months after our January 2027 launch, or upon reaching 1,500,000 active subscribers — whichever comes first.
How do I become an investor or sponsor?
Email investors@milliontribe.com or sponsors@milliontribe.com with your details and we will be in touch within 48 hours.
Where is Million Tribe based?
Million Tribe Inc. is a Delaware C-Corporation headquartered in Houston, Texas, USA. We operate globally across 183 countries.
