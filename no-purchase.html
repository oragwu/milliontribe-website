How to Enter for Free

You may enter the Million Tribe monthly $1,000,000 sweepstakes draw at no cost using our Alternative Method of Entry (AMOE). Simply send a handwritten mail-in entry following the steps below.

1

Handwrite Your Entry

On a plain 3" x 5" index card or piece of paper, handwrite your full legal name, complete mailing address, email address, date of birth, and phone number. Typed or printed entries are not accepted — the entry must be handwritten.

2

Place in an Envelope

Insert your handwritten entry card into a standard mailing envelope. Seal the envelope securely. Each envelope may contain only one entry. Multiple entries in a single envelope will be disqualified.

3

Affix Proper Postage

Affix sufficient first-class postage to the envelope. Entries sent without proper postage will not be accepted. Million Tribe is not responsible for lost, late, misdirected, or undeliverable mail.

4

Mail to the Address Below

Mail your entry to arrive before the entry deadline for the applicable monthly draw period. One free mail-in entry per person per monthly draw period.

Entry Deadlines

Each monthly draw covers one calendar month. To be eligible for a specific month's draw, your mail-in entry must be received (not postmarked) by the 25th day of that calendar month.

Example: To enter the January 2027 draw, your entry must be received by January 25, 2027. Entries received after this date will be entered into the following month's draw, at Million Tribe's sole discretion.

Draw dates are the last day of each calendar month. The winner will be announced within 48 hours of the draw.
